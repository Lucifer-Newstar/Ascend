# ASCEND 1.1.0 release notes

Released 2026-10-23 from `main`, tag `v1.1.0`. Full list of changes:
[CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md#110---2026-10-23).

## Highlights

- **One trainer, one chat.** Pick your trainer (four to choose from) and open the chat from the
  top bar. It answers app questions from help notes, checks goals, gives you a tour of any page
  and explains why it posted what it posted. The separate Coach page is gone; its weekly review
  and deload cards are on Progress.
- **Changes only when you say so.** The chat, the command bar and the local AI can propose a log
  entry, a program switch or an erase; it runs only after Confirm on a card (erase needs the word
  typed), with an audit log and Undo. One shared allowlist defines what may be proposed.
- **A trainer that reads you.** What the trainer brings up, and your daily quest, follow your
  level, readiness, consistency and diet: fewer sets on a low-readiness day, protein or meal
  logging when that is your gap, and nothing hard or eat-less when it would not be safe.
- **Planner.** Build a training week for your days, minutes and equipment, a day of Indian meals
  for your diet, and check whether a goal is realistic against ICMR-NIN 2024 rates. Under 18 it
  never plans a deficit, and **Hide calories** keeps kcal numbers off screen.
- **Profile, photos and sharing.** A Hunter card with your stats and titles, private progress
  photos (metadata stripped, blurred until you reveal them, backed up), and share cards you can
  save, copy, post through X's own composer or take to Instagram. Cards never show weight,
  photos or profile details.
- **More skills.** Five new trees (17 built in) and your own custom trees from library exercises.
- **One first run.** Onboarding, your trainer, then a five-step tour.

## Install

1. Download **`ASCEND-Setup-1.1.0.exe`** from the assets below.
2. Run it. The installer is **not code-signed**, so Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected
   your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose a folder (default `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\ASCEND`; no admin rights needed) and finish.

**Upgrading** from 1.0.0: run the new installer over the old one. Your data in `%APPDATA%\ASCEND`
is kept; the app backs up the database before it migrates it (`0019` → `0025`). On first start
you see a short welcome that offers the tour.

## Verify the download

```powershell
Get-FileHash .\ASCEND-Setup-1.1.0.exe -Algorithm SHA256
```

The hash must match the line for `ASCEND-Setup-1.1.0.exe` in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Privacy and security notes

- Progress photos stay in your data folder. The app re-encodes them on import (no EXIF, no GPS)
  and the engine strips metadata again; they are included in backups. AI photo feedback runs
  only on a local vision model, is off by default, is unavailable under 18 and never comments on
  body fat or weight.
- Share cards are drawn on your PC. "Post on X" opens X's post composer in your browser with the
  caption filled in; nothing is posted for you. The app can open no other site this way.
- Three new Electron IPC channels (`image:save`, `clipboard:write`, `share:open`) are validated
  in the main process; see [docs/contracts/ipc-contract.md](../contracts/ipc-contract.md).
- The demo-data seeder is no longer packaged into the engine, and CI fails a build that
  contains it.

## Known limitations

- **Unsigned installer.** SmartScreen warns until the project has a code-signing certificate.
- **Backups are unencrypted**, and now include progress photos. Keep the backups folder private
  and copy it to another drive yourself.
- **No food photo recognition in the app.** Food is logged by search; the classifier pipeline in
  `ml/` has only been run on synthetic smoke data.
- **AI photo feedback and AI chat wording are not tested against real models.** Tests use a
  mocked Ollama; at run time the number guard and the photo guard filter the replies.
- **Accuracy numbers come from simulated users**, not real people
  ([docs/ai/evaluation.md](../ai/evaluation.md)).
- Instagram has no desktop posting route: the app saves the image and copies the caption for you
  to post from your phone.
- Custom skills pay no XP, so XP stays comparable between users.
- Food values are approximate; ASCEND is not medical or dietary advice.
