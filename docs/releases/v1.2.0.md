# ASCEND 1.2.0 release notes

Built on the `v12-test` branch and tested on a real PC before it is merged to `main`. Full list
of changes: [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md#120---2026-10-23).

## Highlights

- **It looks and feels like a game.** A System intro screen, five new app themes (Shadow is the
  default) next to the old Light and Dark looks, a big trainer with moods, an avatar that ranks
  up E to S, a cosmetics catalog, boss tiers and four seasonal bosses.
- **Simpler.** Today shows only your quest, workout, streak and boss; Settings has six sections;
  the command bar and the trainer chat are one box; plain words everywhere.
- **Your kit decides.** The loadout you pick filters programs, exercises, skills, quests and
  bosses. Every section has Beginner, Intermediate and Expert programs, or one generated for you,
  or your own, and Today follows it.
- **Moves you can see.** Every move loops between its start and end photo with three form tips,
  the most used ones play a proper animation, and the full-screen timer keeps the screen awake.
- **Kinder streaks.** Miss a day, tell the trainer why by the next day, and the streak stays.
- **Weekly recap.** A short video of your week, made on your PC, that you can save.
- **India's food guide.** The ICMR-NIN 2024 guidelines in plain words, with a check of today's
  plate. Body-neutral: it never changes your targets and never talks about your body.
- **Photo safety.** Progress photos stay on your PC, blur when you leave the app, can be left out
  of a backup, and can all be deleted in one step.

## Install or upgrade

1. In the app you have now: **Settings > Data & backups > Back up now**.
2. Run **`ASCEND-Setup-1.2.0.exe`** over the old version. The installer is not code-signed, so
   Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run
   anyway**.
3. Your data in `%APPDATA%\ASCEND` is kept. The app backs up the database before it migrates it
   (`0025` → `0031`). Existing players keep the Dark look; new players start on Shadow.

Everything runs offline. The optional local AI stays off unless you install a model.
