# ASCEND 1.3.0 release notes

Built on the `feat/v13-*` branches and tested on a real PC before it is merged to `main`. Full
list of changes: [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md#130---2026-10-25). The rules this release was
checked against: [RULES_AND_BASELINE.md](../RULES_AND_BASELINE.md).

## Highlights

- **Ask the trainer to change things.** Goal, kit, theme, training days, rest times or the whole
  program, in plain words. You always get a card first; confirm, cancel or undo it, also by
  typing. Works without the AI.
- **Log by typing.** "3x10 push-ups, plank 45 s" or "2 idli and sambar" become one card. Foods
  that are missing can be added on the spot.
- **Kinder streaks.** Only training keeps the streak, your program's rest days never break it,
  and a missed day with a reason counts as rest. Coming back after a gap earns a welcome-back
  bonus.
- **Goals within reach.** Today shows sessions done and the next milestone, your next sub-rank,
  your best streak and weeks in a row. These numbers never go down.
- **Calm reminders.** Two a day at most, none on rest days, no "your streak ends at midnight".
  Extra nudges are opt-in.
- **No AI judging your body.** The AI photo feedback is gone. Photos stay on your PC for you to
  compare side by side.
- **Chat page** with the big trainer portrait and two new trainers, Tara and Idris.

## Install or upgrade

1. In the app you have now: **Settings > Data & backups > Back up now**.
2. Run **`ASCEND-Setup-1.3.0.exe`** over the old version. The installer is not code-signed, so
   Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run
   anyway**.
3. Your data in `%APPDATA%\ASCEND` is kept. The app backs up the database before it migrates it
   (`0031` → `0032`). Items you unlocked with a streak stay unlocked.

Everything runs offline. The optional local AI stays off unless you install a model.

## Good to know

- The evening check, the monthly photo reminder and the weigh-in nudge start **off**; turn them on
  in Settings if you want them.
- The five hunter stats moved from Today to Profile.
- A done trainer card offers **Undo**, which puts the old value back ([how trainer actions work](../ai/trainer-actions.md)).
