# Anna University R2021 project rules and review windows

Checked against the official sources on 22 November 2026. This is a short reference for guide discussion, not confirmation of an individual student's registered regulation or the college's actual review/viva booking.

## Published framework

Use the [approved amended R2021 regulations for affiliated non-autonomous institutions](https://cac.annauniv.edu/aidetails/reg/Approved%20R2021/Undergraduate%20Programme%20-%20Academic%20Regulations%202021.pdf), linked by the [Centre for Academic Courses](https://cac.annauniv.edu/). Older R2021 PDFs still indexed online contain a different project split; do not combine them with the amendments. The summary below follows amended clause 12.4, especially page 21.

- Project Work is registered in the final semester. It may be individual or a group of **at most four** students (12.4).
- A qualified department teacher supervises the work; the regulation defines this as a faculty member with a PG degree or PhD (12.4.1).
- The review committee includes the supervisor, a department expert and project coordinator, with an alternate if roles overlap (12.4.3).
- There are **three progress reviews, 20 marks each**, totalling **60 internal marks**. Each student presents their progress (12.4.3-12.4.4).
- The final **report carries 10 marks**, awarded by the external examiner, with the same report marks for the group.
- The **individual viva carries 30 marks**: 10 each from the internal examiner, external examiner and supervisor. The mark depends on each student's performance (12.4.4). University project examiners are appointed by the Controller of Examinations (11.6).
- The report deadline is the semester's last working day (12.4.5). Get the college's operational submission instructions early.

This note does not invent a mandatory publication, numerical similarity threshold, slide count, presentation duration or AI-detector rule. Confirm the current department requirements and permitted assistance/disclosure with the guide.

The project-work row and main semester dates were visually checked in the PDF.

| Milestone | Published date/window |
|---|---|
| Classes commence | 21 December 2026 |
| Review 1 period | 21 December 2026 to 11 February 2027 |
| Review 2 period | 12 February to 19 March 2027 |
| Review 3 period | 22 March to 30 April 2027 |
| Last working day | 30 April 2027 |
| End-semester examinations commence | 5 May 2027 |

These are university windows, not the college's booked review days. The examination commencement date is not an individual project-viva appointment. Ask the guide/project coordinator for the actual slots and current report template.
